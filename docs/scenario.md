# Scenario and lab rules

Adria Motori d.o.o. is a fictional importer and distributor of vehicles, with offices in Zagreb and Ljubljana and a dealer network run by independent partners. The story has about 25 staff; 14 of them exist in the tenant.

I play the newly hired AI solutions and security lead. The other roles are played by an AI assistant:

| Person | Role in the story |
| --- | --- |
| Dario Novak | Regional IT lead, my manager |
| Goran Vidović | Chief executive, approves company-wide policies |
| Mirela Kovač, Ana Šimić, Tomislav Barić, Ivana Horvat, Sanja Zupan | Department heads who send requests |
| Nina Babić, Marko Jurišić, Lea Knežević, Jure Matić | AI champions in sales, after-sales, finance and marketing |

A second, smaller fictional company without Microsoft 365 follows later, to practise the same work with fewer tools.

## Rules

1. Nothing real. Fictional company, people and data; never an employer's tenant, real customers or real guests.
2. Secrets never enter a chat or this repository. Documents use placeholders in UPPER_CASE; real values live in files only the owner can read.
3. Billing settings are not touched during a trial (see decision D-004).

## How a ticket runs

1. A request arrives, usually vague, the way real requests do.
2. I ask the questions that change what gets built: who owns it, what goes in, where it lives, what already exists, who approves, by when.
3. One or two sentences on the approach.
4. The build, step by step in the portal or in PowerShell.
5. A deliverable the requester can check.
6. A review, in character, which usually finds something.
7. The CISSP concepts the ticket exercised and five exam-style questions.
8. An entry in the decision log.
