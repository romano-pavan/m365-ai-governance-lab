# Data traffic light for AI tools

The policy page as approved for employees of the fictional Adria Motori, in Croatian, followed by an English translation. The mapping to sensitivity labels and the reasoning are in [docs/L01-ai-register.md](../docs/L01-ai-register.md).

## Hrvatski (objavljeno)

### Semafor: koji podaci smiju u koji AI alat

Prije nego što nešto zalijepiš ili učitaš u AI alat, pogledaj boju podatka.

| Boja | Podaci | Gdje smiju |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ZELENO | Javno: cjenici, katalozi, objave, sve što je već na našem webu | U bilo koji AI alat, i besplatni |
| ŽUTO | Interno i Povjerljivo: interne upute, mailovi, ponude, ugovori s partnerima, marže, podaci kupaca kad ih posao traži | Samo u odobrene alate s popisa ispod, uz prijavu službenim računom |
| CRVENO | Strogo povjerljivo: plaće, zdravlje i bolovanja, kadrovski postupci, lozinke i ključevi, brojevi kartica i računa | Ni u jedan AI alat, osim rješenja koje je u registru odobreno baš za te podatke |

### Odobreni alati

Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat (m365.cloud.microsoft/chat), prijava službenim računom. Do žute razine.

Osobni i besplatni ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude i slični alati: samo za zelene podatke.

Jedna iznimka: AI ne odlučuje o ljudima. Zapošljavanje, ocjenjivanje i nagrade idu preko IT-a prije bilo kakvog alata.

Ne znaš koja je boja? Tretiraj kao crveno i pitaj championa svog odjela ili Romana Pavana.

Vlasnik: Romano Pavan, IT. Odobrio: Goran Vidović. Verzija 1, listopad 2026.

## English translation

### Traffic light: which data may go into which AI tool

Before you paste or upload anything into an AI tool, check the colour of the data.

| Colour | Data | Where it may go |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GREEN | Public: price lists, catalogues, published posts, anything already on our website | Any AI tool, including free ones |
| YELLOW | Internal and Confidential: internal procedures, emails, quotes, partner contracts, margins, customer data when the work requires it | Only approved tools from the list below, signed in with the work account |
| RED | Strictly confidential: salaries, health and sick leave, HR proceedings, passwords and keys, card and account numbers | No AI tool, except a solution approved in the register for exactly this data |

### Approved tools

Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat (m365.cloud.microsoft/chat), signed in with the work account. Up to yellow.

Personal and free ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and similar tools: green data only.

One exception: AI does not decide about people. Hiring, appraisals and rewards go through IT before any tool is used.

Not sure which colour it is? Treat it as red and ask your department's champion or Romano Pavan.

Owner: Romano Pavan, IT. Approved by: Goran Vidović. Version 1, October 2026.
